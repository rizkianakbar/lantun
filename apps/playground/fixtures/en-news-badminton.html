Jakarta crowd roars as home pair claim doubles title in three games

SportsWire staff21 June 2026, 19:40 WIB

JAKARTA, June 21 – A packed Istora Senayan erupted on Sunday as Indonesia's top men's doubles pair came from a game down to win the Indonesia Open final 19-21, 21-17, 21-15 in just over an hour.

The pair, seeded fourth, trailed 11-6 at the interval of the second game before a run of seven straight points swung the match. Their opponents, the world No. 2 pair from Denmark, had won their last three meetings.

"When the crowd is like this, you don't feel tired," one of the winners said afterwards. "We were nervous in the first game. Then we just decided to attack every shuttle."

The win is Indonesia's first men's doubles title at the tournament in three years. Earlier in the day, the women's singles final went to a three-game thriller, with China's second seed edging Japan's defending champion 22-20 in the decider.

The tour moves on to the Australian Open in Sydney next week.
