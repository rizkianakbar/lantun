A page with very aggressive global styles

This page resets every element with all: unset, forces huge pink buttons and lime SVGs, and pins its own elements to the maximum z-index. A well-isolated widget should still look and behave exactly as it does everywhere else.

It should also still find this article, split it into sentences, and highlight words as they are read aloud. If you can read this sentence with a soft highlight behind it, the highlighting survived too.
